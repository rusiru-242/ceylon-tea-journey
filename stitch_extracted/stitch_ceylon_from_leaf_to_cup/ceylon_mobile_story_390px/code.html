THE ART OF CEYLON TEA

FROM NATURE TO PERFECTION.

A journey through the misty landscapes, generational craftsmanship, and traditions behind every cup.

CHAPTER 02 — THE LEAF

Nature's Finest Detail.

Every exceptional cup begins with the freshness of a tender tea shoot. Two leaves and a bud, kissed by mountain dew.

CHAPTER 03 — THE HARVEST

Picked with Purpose.

Skilled hands carefully select the youngest leaves, placed inside woven baskets to protect delicate aromatics.

CHAPTER 04 — THE FACTORY

Crafted with Care.

Fresh leaves enter a world of time-honored craftsmanship and precise processing under warm industrial factory lighting.

CHAPTER 05 — WITHERING

The Art of Transformation.

Airflow gradually reduces moisture, preparing the supple leaves for rolling while releasing intense botanical aromas.

CHAPTER 06 — ROLLING

Crafted Through Motion.

Rolling twists the softened leaves, preparing them to develop their distinctive character and releasing essential oils.

CHAPTER 07 — OXIDATION

Where Character Develops.

Natural enzymatic oxidation helps develop the rich color, aroma, and flavor of black tea as leaves turn warm copper-auburn.

CHAPTER 08 — DRYING AND GRADING

Precision in Every Leaf.

Controlled drying preserves the tea, while precision grading separates finished leaves by orthodox size.

CHAPTER 09 — WHITE TRANSITION

From Craft to Excellence.

Dark finished tea leaves emerge onto pure white studio space—isolated like an architectural luxury monograph.

CHAPTER 10 — TEA EXPERIENCE

A Richer Experience.

The finished tea reveals its beautiful amber color and distinctive character inside pure porcelain.

CHAPTER 11 — PACKAGING

Carefully Preserved.

Airtight ivory containers lock in the delicate mountain aromatics and pristine quality of every harvest.

CHAPTER 12 — BREWING

The Perfect Infusion.

Crystal glass reveals the amber infusion as whole leaves unfurl and surrender their highland essence.

EVERY CUP HAS A JOURNEY.

From the misty highlands of Sri Lanka to a moment of pure perfection.
