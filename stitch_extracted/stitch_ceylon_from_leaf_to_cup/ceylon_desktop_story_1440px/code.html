Picked with Purpose.

CHAPTER 04 — THE FACTORY

Crafted with Care.

Fresh leaves enter a world of time-honored craftsmanship and precise processing. Warm industrial lighting guides generational tea artisans through each stage.

Historic Ceylon Estate • Generational Masters
CHAPTER 05 — WITHERING

The Art of Transformation.

Airflow gradually reduces moisture, preparing the leaves for rolling. Leaves become supple and pliable, releasing a rich verdant bouquet.

Moisture Reduction: 55% • Controlled Ambient Flow
CHAPTER 06 — ROLLING

Crafted Through Motion.

Rolling twists the softened leaves, preparing them to develop their distinctive character. The delicate cellular structure ruptures to release essential oils.

Circular Mechanical Rolling • Cellular Rupture
CHAPTER 07 — OXIDATION

Where Character Develops.

Natural enzymatic oxidation helps develop the rich color, aroma, and flavor of black tea as leaves turn toward copper-auburn.

Enzymatic Alchemy • Copper Color Transition
CHAPTER 08 — DRYING AND GRADING

Precision in Every Leaf.

Controlled drying preserves the tea, while grading separates the finished leaves by size. The final factory stage produces black tea of extraordinary clarity.

Moisture Lock: 3% • Orthodox Leaf Grading
